# Review Policy

- Keep the original file untouched and write derived artifacts beside it.
- Cite a precise official or user-supplied source for every non-`通过` conclusion.
- Downgrade uncertain applicability, inaccessible sources, or ambiguous text matches to `信息不足` and request human review.
- Do not upload complete enterprise documents to unconfigured services; redact sensitive excerpts in logs.
- State that the result is an assistive compliance review, not legal advice or a guarantee of completeness.
